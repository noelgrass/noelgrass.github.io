Can I rent a film score for a Kenyan production?

Yes. Noel Grass licenses his original cinematic scores directly to Kenyan directors and production houses. Tracks are available for film, TV, documentary, and commercial productions — in Kenya, East Africa, and internationally.

What is the difference between renting a master recording and licensing a composition?

Renting the master recording gives the producer permission to use the audio file in a specific project. The composition rights — the underlying music and melody — remain with the composer, Noel Grass.

Do I need PAVRISK to license music for a film in Kenya?

No. When licensing directly from the composer who owns the rights, no collective management organization is required. PAVRISK handles public performance royalties for music played in public spaces, not direct sync licenses.

Are these film scores available for East African and international productions?

Yes. The catalogue is licensed for productions across Kenya, East Africa, the wider African continent, and international markets.

How much does it cost to rent a film score?

Film score licensing starts at Ksh 10,000 per track for a single production. International licensing is available in USD, GBP, or EUR — contact for rates. Full terms are available on the Rent page.
