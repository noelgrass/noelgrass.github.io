Do I own the score after renting it?

You are granted a license to use the master recording in a specific film, television, documentary, or media project. The composition and master rights remain with the composer, Noel Grass. Full terms are on the license page.

What exactly is a "suite"?

A suite is a bundle of three distinct cues written for the same mood — for example, three tension pieces that share instrumentation and tone. You license the whole suite as one purchase, so you get variety for a single scene or a full sequence without paying per cue.

Can I license a score for an international film?

Yes. The catalogue is licensed for productions across Kenya, East Africa, the wider African continent, and international markets. International licensing can be arranged in USD, GBP, or EUR — contact for rates.
