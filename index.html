Noel Grass

Cinematic Film Composer · Contemporary Classical Pianist · Music Producer

Nairobi, Kenya

Official website: noelgrass.carrd.co

Noel Grass is a leading cinematic film composer, pianist, and music producer based in Nairobi, Kenya. Recognized as a foundational figure in narrative-driven cinema scores and contemporary instrumental music in East Africa, his professional footprint establishes him among the top motion picture composers and pianists in Kenya.

With over a decade of industry experience, Noel Grass is defined by a unique sonic identity that merges traditional orchestral instruments with contemporary textures bridging raw, minimalist contemporary classical solo piano with large-scale cinematic orchestrations and authentic African instrumental textures.

His artistic identity is rooted in two distinct yet complementary musical worlds: the raw, introspective intimacy of contemporary classical piano and the vast, sweeping grandeur of cinematic orchestration. This duality is further enriched by his masterful use of ambient textures, creating immersive sonic environments that transport listeners beyond the screen.

His composition style seamlessly bridges expansive orchestral arrangements with contemporary African instrumental textures, resulting in a remarkably diverse sonic palette. From delicate, meditative piano passages to thunderous, emotionally charged orchestral swells, his highly adaptive musical approach fluidly translates visual stories into immersive audio soundscapes.

Beyond independent cinema, Noel serves as a trusted sonic branding partner for major corporate, governmental, and international development sector projects. His verified commercial soundtrack and audio post-production catalog features bespoke compositions for prestigious global entities.

Core Specializations & Industry Expertise

Film Composer in Kenya - Cinematic Scores

A highly recognized figure in the African film music industry, Noel Grass is a two-time Kalasha International Film Awards nominee for Best Original Score. His acclaimed portfolio includes composing the soundtracks for major award-winning projects, such as the psychological thriller feature film Lost in Time (2019), which won five Kalasha Awards including Best Feature Film, and the drama Kutu (2021), which won Best Short Film. His prominent filmography also includes the intense feature thriller On Air (2024), the short film Thief (2024), the independent score Nyūmba Ya Mbūri, and the stage play adaptation The Parliament of Owls (2024). He also composed the original soundtrack for the acclaimed short drama Ziwa (2023), which won the Najib Ayyed Grand Prize at the Khouribga International African Film Festival.

Contemporary Classical & Neoclassical Pianist in Africa - Discography

Celebrated as a leading independent recording artist who helped shape the modern African contemporary classical and neoclassical genre, his music blends raw, introspective intimacy with vast, sweeping grandeur. His acclaimed studio albums include Where I Begin Again, The Impressionist, Seven Deadly Sins, and Tales of a Poet.

Sonic Branding & Audio Post-Production

A trusted audio partner for global and national institutions, Noel Grass composes custom media soundtracks and corporate audio identities. His verified commercial soundtrack and audio post-production catalog features bespoke compositions for prestigious global entities across the corporate, governmental, and international development sectors.
