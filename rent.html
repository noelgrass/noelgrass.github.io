Rent / License

Original music for productions.

New to Licensing? Renting / licensing music. Here's what it actually means. ↓ Click to open — what's included and what you can do with it

When you rent / license music from this catalogue, you are buying the right to use it in your production, a license. You do not own the music. Noel Grass still owns it. But you receive a signed, legal permission to use the track exactly as agreed.

When you complete your download, your license is included with your files. You can check the full licensing terms anytime to review the details of your agreement.

What You Receive
  • The high-quality audio file, the master, ready to drop into your edit.
  • A written license stating exactly what you can and cannot do with it.
  • Confirmation of the terms: duration, territory, and permitted uses.
What You Can Use It For
  • Films, shorts, documentaries, and series.
  • TV, streaming, and online distribution.
  • Brand films, advertisements, and campaigns.
  • Podcasts, trailers, and promotional pieces.
What You Can't Do Without Upgrading
  • Resell the music on its own as a standalone track.
  • Claim the composition as your own work.
  • Use it in more projects than your license covers.
How It Works
  1. Browse the catalogue and preview the tracks.
  2. Choose the track and confirm the license terms for your project.
  3. Pay, then you receive the master file and the written license.
  4. Drop it into your production. Credit Noel Grass where reasonable.

That's the whole process. If you're unsure what license fits your project, tell me what you're making and I'll guide you.
