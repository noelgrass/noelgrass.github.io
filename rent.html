New to Licensing? Renting music isn't buying it. Here's what it actually means. When you rent music from this catalogue, you are buying the right to use it in your production, a licence. You do not own the music. Noel still owns it. But you receive a signed, legal permission to use the track exactly as agreed: for a specific project, in a specific territory, for a specific period of time.
What You Receive
  • The high-quality audio file, the master, ready to drop into your edit.
  • A written licence stating exactly what you can and cannot do with it.
  • Confirmation of the terms: duration, territory, and permitted uses.
What You Can Use It For
  • Films, shorts, documentaries, and series.
  • TV, streaming, and online distribution.
  • Brand films, advertisements, and campaigns.
  • Podcasts, trailers, and promotional pieces.
What You Can't Do Without Upgrading
  • Resell the music on its own as a standalone track.
  • Claim the composition as your own work.
  • Use it in more projects than your licence covers.
How It Works
  1. Browse the catalogue and preview the tracks.
  2. Choose the track and confirm the licence terms for your project.
  3. Pay, then you receive the master file and the written licence.
  4. Drop it into your production. Credit Noel Grass where reasonable.

That's the whole process. If you're unsure what licence fits your project, tell me what you're making and I'll guide you.
