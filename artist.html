About the Artist

Noel Grass is a neoclassical pianist, contemporary classical composer, orchestrator, arranger, and music producer based in Nairobi, Kenya. His practice moves across solo piano, orchestral composition, hybrid scoring, and ambient sound design from the intimacy of a single instrument to the sweep of a full ensemble.

As a pianist and composer, his solo works Into A Stillness That Answers, The Impressionist trilogy, and Where I Begin Again sit within the contemporary classical tradition. They draw on impressionism, cinematic texture, and the restraint of neoclassical form, without settling into any single school.

As an orchestrator, arranger, and producer, he shapes each release from the studio building arrangements, layering traditional orchestral instruments with ambient textures and African instrumental elements, and producing the final work. His practice is built on a single principle: emotion directs the architecture.

Four Modes

His catalogue is organised around four distinct sonic modes:

  • Solo Piano — neoclassical and contemporary classical piano, at its most intimate.
  • Orchestral — full ensemble writing, from sweeping cinematic to introspective chamber.
  • Hybrid — piano and orchestration interwoven with electronic textures and sound design.
  • Ambient — layered, atmospheric work built around texture and tone.
Where He Works

Based in Nairobi, Noel Grass writes, records, and produces from Kenya. His work is released internationally and licensed for productions across Kenya, East Africa, pan-African markets, and internationally spanning recorded solo releases, film composition, and commissioned work.

The catalogue is available for streaming worldwide, and individual works can be licensed for film, television, brand, and broadcast productions.
