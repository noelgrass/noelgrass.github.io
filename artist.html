Noel Grass — Neoclassical Pianist, Contemporary Classical Composer, Orchestrator, Music Arranger & Music Producer

Neoclassical Pianist  ·  Contemporary Classical Composer  ·  Orchestrator  ·  Music Arranger  ·  Music Producer

About the Artist

Noel Grass writes music for piano and for orchestra, for a single instrument and for a full ensemble. His work moves between the quiet of solo piano and the expanse of cinematic sound, always searching for the space where emotion and architecture meet.

His solo works, Into A Stillness That Answers, The Impressionist trilogy, and Where I Begin Again, sit within the contemporary classical tradition. They lean on impressionism, cinematic texture, and the restraint of neoclassical form, without ever settling into a single school.

In the studio, he shapes each release himself, layering traditional orchestral instruments with ambient textures and African instrumental elements. His practice is built on a single principle: emotion directs the architecture.

Four Rooms

His catalogue moves through four sonic spaces:

  • Solo Piano. Just the instrument, in a quiet room.
  • Orchestral. Full ensemble writing, from sweeping to still.
  • Hybrid. Piano and orchestra woven with electronic textures.
  • Ambient. Layered atmosphere, built from tone and time.
Where He Works

Noel Grass writes, records, and produces from Kenya. His work is released internationally, licensed across East Africa, pan-African markets, and beyond, and commissioned for film, television, and broadcast. He is equally at home in the studio and on an international stage.

The catalogue is available for streaming worldwide, and individual works can be licensed for film, television, brand, and broadcast productions.
