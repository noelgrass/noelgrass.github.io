Can I rent a jingle for a Kenyan TV or radio commercial?

Yes. Noel Grass licenses original jingles directly to Kenyan brands, agencies, and production houses for TV, radio, online, and broadcast advertising campaigns. The catalogue is also licensed for East African, pan-African, and international productions.

Do I own the jingle after renting it?

You are granted a license to use the master recording in a specific campaign or project. The composition and master rights remain with the composer, Noel Grass.

Can I rent a jingle for an East African or international campaign?

Yes. The catalogue is licensed for campaigns across Kenya, East Africa, the wider African continent, and international markets.

How much does it cost to rent a jingle?

Jingle licensing starts at Ksh 5,000 per track for a single campaign. International licensing is available in USD, GBP, or EUR. Contact for rates. Full terms are available on the Rent page.
