Film Composer

Cinematic Music Composition & Audio Production

I am Noel Grass, a film composer and pianist based in Nairobi, Kenya. I write music for picture. Cinematic stories need sound that carries their weight, and my work is to find it. The scores I have written have shaped a single principle: emotion first.

Some stories need stillness. Others need movement. My job is to read which one the scene is asking for, and build the sound around that.

The Scored Scene

This is work in service of the picture. It is an act of shared vision, translating the emotional core of independent films, features, and documentaries into sound. The goal is simple: for the score to work as an unseen character within the frame.

Notable Filmography. Lost in Time (2019), a feature film that won Best Feature Film. Kutu, a short film that won the Kalasha Award for Best Short Film.

Commercial Music & Sonic Branding

Jingles, TV and radio commercial music, brand anthems, and full custom soundtracks for corporate and international development campaigns. Clients have included the International Finance Corporation (IFC / World Bank Group), the Higher Education Loans Board (HELB), the Government Advertising Agency (GAA), and SOMINVEST.

Already-made scores and jingles can also be licensed from the catalogue. See the Rent page.

The Consistent Method

Whether for screen or speaker, the method is the same. I design with enough intention that you do not just hear the piece. You find yourself inside it.

Can I license existing music instead of commissioning new work?

Yes. Already-made scores and jingles from the Noel Grass catalogue can be licensed for immediate use. This is faster than commissioning a new piece and suits productions with tight timelines or smaller budgets. See the Rent page for the licensing catalogue.

What does a film composer do?

A film composer writes original music for a film, documentary, or brand campaign. The score supports the story, sets tone, and shapes how the audience feels at each moment. Noel Grass composes cinematic scores that function as an unseen character within the frame.

What should I look for in a film composer?

Look for a composer with a clear process, demonstrated range, and a track record you can hear. Strong communication and deadline reliability matter as much as the music itself. Noel Grass brings a four-step process, awards for Best Feature Film and Best Short Film, and a client list that includes the IFC and World Bank Group.

Where is Noel Grass based?

Noel Grass is based in Nairobi, Kenya. He works with clients across East Africa and internationally, from feature films and documentaries to commercial campaigns and sonic branding.
