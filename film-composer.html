Noel Grass ·

Film Composer

Cinematic Music Composition & Audio Production

Noel Grass is a film composer and pianist based in Nairobi, Kenya. He writes music for picture. Cinematic stories need sound that carries their weight, and his work is to find it. The scores he has written have shaped a single principle: emotion first.

Some stories need stillness. Others need movement. His job is to read which one the scene is asking for, and build the sound around that.

The Scored Scene

This is work in service of the picture. It is an act of shared vision, translating the emotional core of independent films, features, and documentaries into sound. The goal is simple: for the score to work as an unseen character within the frame.

Noel Grass is a two-time Kalasha International Film Awards nominee for Best Original Score. His portfolio includes the psychological thriller feature film Lost in Time (2019), which won five Kalasha Awards including Best Feature Film, and the drama Kutu (2021), which won Best Short Film. He composed the original soundtrack for the acclaimed short drama Ziwa (2023), which won the Najib Ayyed Grand Prize at the Khouribga International African Film Festival. He also scored the intense feature thriller On Air (2024), the short film Thief (2024), the independent score Nyūmba Ya Mbūri, and the stage play adaptation The Parliament of Owls (2024).

What does a film composer do?

A film composer writes original music for a film, documentary, or brand campaign. The score supports the story, sets tone, and shapes how the audience feels at each moment. Noel Grass composes cinematic scores that function as an unseen character within the frame.

What should I look for in a film composer?

Look for a composer with a clear process, demonstrated range, and a track record you can hear. Strong communication and deadline reliability matter as much as the music itself.
