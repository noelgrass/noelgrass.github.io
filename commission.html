Philosophy

My work starts with your story. The core rule is simple: the emotion of the scene decides the music. The score isn't just an addition; it's a foundational layer built from the emotional needs of your project.

The Process

To protect the creative vision, I follow a clear, four-step path:

  1. The Brief: We define the core feeling, the story's mood, and the project's practical boundaries.
  2. The Agreement: We formalize the brief, deliverables, timeline, and terms in writing. No creative work starts without this step.
  3. The Creation: I compose and send drafts as the score develops. You give feedback, and I refine. This is where the music takes shape.
  4. The Delivery: You receive the final piece as a polished, intentional work, ready for its purpose.
