Commission a Scene — Original Film Scoring in Kenya and Worldwide

The process for commissioning an original music project for film, media, or a custom sonic space.

Based in Nairobi, Kenya, I work with filmmakers, production houses, and agencies across East Africa and internationally — from feature films and documentaries to commercial campaigns and sonic branding.

Philosophy

My work starts with your story. The core rule is simple: the emotion of the scene decides the music. The score isn't just an addition; it's a foundational layer built from the emotional needs of your project.

The Process

To protect the creative vision, I follow a clear, four-step path:

1. The Brief: We define the core feeling, the story's mood, and the project's practical boundaries.
2. The Agreement: We formalize the brief, deliverables, timeline, and terms in writing. No creative work starts without this step.
3. The Creation: I compose and produce the score privately, following our brief.
4. The Delivery: You receive the final piece as a polished, intentional work, ready for its purpose.
The Foundation

Every commission follows a non-negotiable standard to ensure the music has clarity, intentional pacing, and fits seamlessly into its world. This is a collaboration built on trust and a shared vision.
