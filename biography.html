Noel Grass

Cinematic Film Composer · Contemporary Classical Pianist · Music Producer

Nairobi, Kenya  ·  Worldwide

Official website: noelgrass.com

Noel Grass writes music for the screen and for the room. His scores move with the story, whether in a darkened cinema or in the quiet space of a solo piano album, and his work has become part of how East African cinema sounds.

Over more than a decade, he has built a sound that merges traditional orchestral instruments with contemporary textures, holding together the raw intimacy of solo piano and the sweep of large-scale cinematic orchestration, with African instrumental voices woven through both. His compositional voice was shaped early by performing across rap, hip-hop, jazz, and Latin ensembles, an experience that informs the rhythmic and textural range of his work, even as his own music remains rooted in the contemporary classical tradition.

His work lives between two worlds. One is the introspective intimacy of contemporary classical piano. The other is the expanse of cinematic sound. Ambient textures sit between them, holding the listener inside the work long after the screen goes dark.

From delicate, meditative piano passages to orchestral swells built for the emotional weight of a scene, his writing translates visual stories into sound. The palette is wide, but the principle is simple: every arrangement serves the picture.

Beyond cinema, he writes for institutions. His commercial and audio post-production catalogue includes bespoke work for global development, government, and corporate clients, where the sound has to carry identity, not just atmosphere.

The Pianist & Composer

Celebrated as a leading independent recording artist who helped shape the modern African contemporary classical and neoclassical genre, his music blends raw, introspective intimacy with vast, sweeping grandeur. His acclaimed studio albums include Where I Begin Again, The Impressionist, Seven Deadly Sins, and Tales of a Poet.

As a Pianist and Composer

As a pianist and composer, Noel Grass works in the contemporary classical and neoclassical traditions. His solo catalogue, which includes Into A Stillness That Answers, The Impressionist trilogy, Where I Begin Again, and Introspect, moves between impressionist restraint, cinematic texture, and the quiet architecture of solo piano writing. Beyond the instrument, his work extends into orchestration, arranging, and production, where traditional orchestral voices are layered with ambient textures and African instrumental elements. His catalogue is organised across four sonic spaces: solo piano, orchestral, experimental, and ambient.

Performance Background

Before his work as a composer and recording artist, Noel Grass spent years performing as a pianist and keyboardist across a wide range of ensembles, from rap and hip-hop groups to jazz combos and Latin bands. That background shaped his instinct for rhythm, groove, and improvisational space, and it continues to inform the way he writes: the classical tradition gives the architecture, but the surrounding experience gives the pulse. It is a range that few composers in the region can claim, and it is part of what gives his music its distinctive contemporary edge.

Film Work

Noel Grass is a two-time Kalasha International Film Awards nominee for Best Original Score, with original scores for feature films, shorts, documentaries, and stage productions. For the full filmography, client list, and awards, see the Film Composer page.
